## Evidence discipline

Keep production reports, production-level prototypes, testbeds, simulations and research tools distinct. Treat author-reported gains as conditional on workloads, baselines and measurement definitions. Interpret competitive relevance as a plausible mechanism rather than a demonstrated moat. Preserve the 32/31 denominator sensitivity, Dorado affiliation conflict and unresolved city/source caveats.
